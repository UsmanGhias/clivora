CLIVORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

CodCrafters ("we", "our") operates the CLIVORA mobile application. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Summary

CLIVORA stores all business data locally on your device. We do not operate a cloud server that collects your customer, project, or invoice data. Your information stays on your phone unless you choose to share it elsewhere.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name and email address when you sign up.
  • Profile photo: Optional image you upload from your camera or gallery.
  • Business data: Customers, projects, invoices, tasks, expenses, and notes you enter in the app.

How We Use Information

  • To provide app functionality (CRM, invoicing, project tracking).
  • To authenticate your account on your device.
  • To display your profile and business dashboard.

Data Storage

All data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Passwords are hashed before storage. We do not transmit your business data to external servers.

Third-Party Services

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts when online. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included in the current version.

Permissions

  • Camera / Photos: To set your profile picture (optional).
  • Internet: To load fonts and future online features.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data by uninstalling CLIVORA or clearing app data in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

CLIVORA is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
