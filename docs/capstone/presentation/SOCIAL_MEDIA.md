## What we always say

- $0 platform fee on the **negotiated deal** (clients pay you directly against the invoice).
- Emails unlock **only after mutual accept**.
- One account for **web and Android**.
- Free / Pro $9 / Pro Plus $19 — **tools subscription**, not a marketplace cut.
- Ask AI is a **Pro** helper. It does not auto-pay invoices.

## What we never say

- We do not take a “small commission” on invoices.
- We are not “Connected” to Google Calendar unless OAuth is actually configured.
- We do not show fake user counts, fake escrow funded, or invented star ratings.
- We do not name-drop Upwork / Fiverr as partners. Independent product only.
- We do not claim Stripe checkout is live if it is still a stub.
- We do not post other people’s invoices, emails, or client names.

## First 12 posts (one a day is enough)

1. **What it is** — Screenshot of clivora.io homepage. Caption: “Hire privately. Deliver the work. Invoice directly. $0 fee on the deal.”
2. **Why** — Founder note: marketplace cuts + five tabs. Keep under 80 words.
3. **Connect** — “Emails stay private until both sides accept.”
4. **Invoice** — Public share link story: send `/i/…`, client pays you.
5. **Android** — Play Store screenshot. “Same login as the website.”
6. **Timesheets** — Timer only when a rate exists. Honest product, not fake busy-ness.
7. **Plans** — Free / Pro $9 / Pro Plus $19 are tools, not a cut of the rate.
8. **Ask AI** — “It can summarise your CRM. It will not auto-pay an invoice.”
9. **How-to** — Carousel: Post a job → accept → project → invoice link.
10. **Community** — Invite to community.clivora.io + contact@clivora.io
11. **Founder face** — You on camera, 20 seconds. Builds trust for the Capstone video too.
12. **Pin** — Repost #1 and pin it.

## Capstone vs marketing

Graders are scoring the **software, sprints, tests, and recorded demo**. Social accounts are extra. Do not spend recording time scrolling Instagram. If a grader asks about GTM, one sentence: “Same brand everywhere; $0 deal fee; web + Android; details in SOCIAL_MEDIA.md.”
