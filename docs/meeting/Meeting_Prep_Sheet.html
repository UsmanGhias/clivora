Questions to Ask Them (Shows Consultant Maturity)

  • Which product line should be the pilot for Phase 1? (roll-off, dumpster, or custom build?)
  • Do you have existing BOMs in spreadsheets today, or are they only in supervisors' heads?
  • How many work centers and shifts per production stage?
  • Is barcode hardware already in use, or starting from scratch?
  • Odoo edition and hosting — Community vs. Enterprise? On-prem vs. Odoo.sh?
  • Target go-live date and preferred budget model (hourly vs. fixed price)?
  • Can you share your internal discovery framework before we start Week 1?
  • Who are the key stakeholders per stage I should interview in Week 1–2?

Objection Handling

If they say...Respond with...
"We've tried ERP before and adoption failed" "That's why I start with discovery and a pilot product line. We keep your existing tracking in parallel for 2–4 weeks. Champions per stage, not big-bang rollout."
"Odoo can't handle our leftover sheet reuse" "Flexible consumption rules, scrap locations, and alternative components handle this. I've configured similar material recovery workflows in batch manufacturing environments."
"We need someone local / on-site" "Discovery Week 1 benefits from on-site walkthrough — I'm available for that. Ongoing config and support work effectively remote with weekly demos and staging access, as proven across 25+ countries."
"What's your rate?" "$22/hour for the discovery phase, $24/hour standard. I recommend capping Phase 1 hours so you have budget certainty before committing to full configuration."
"Do you work solo or with a team?" "I personally lead discovery and configuration. CODCrafters bench is available if scope needs parallel data migration or accelerated rollout."
