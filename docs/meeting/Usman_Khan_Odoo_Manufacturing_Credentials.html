Discovery Methodology

My approach to running discovery with non-technical shop floor staff — structured, respectful of operational expertise, and designed to produce actionable Odoo configuration blueprints.

Week Activities Stakeholders Deliverable
1 Facility walkthrough, order-to-ship shadowing, document current communication methods Production supervisors, shift leads Current-state process swimlanes
2 Workstation interviews — one session per production stage; photo/sketch each station Machine leads, welders, paint crew, assembly team Work center map with capacity per station
3 BOM and material deep dive; purchasing workflows; quality inspection points Admin staff, purchasing, QC team BOM structure document and consumption rules (including leftover sheet reuse)
4 Reporting requirements workshop; adoption planning; pilot product line selection Management, floor representatives Odoo data model blueprint and phased rollout plan

Interview Principles

  • Shop-floor language first — document how supervisors describe their work; translate to Odoo terminology afterward
  • Photo and sketch workstations during walkthroughs to anchor work center definitions in physical reality
  • Validate with a pilot product line before configuring all routings and BOMs
  • Document exceptions explicitly — leftover sheet metal reuse, rush orders, rework loops, custom builds
  • Sign-off gate — no MRP configuration begins until discovery documentation is reviewed and approved by management
  • Respect existing workflows — design Odoo to support how teams actually work, with gradual adoption rather than overnight process overhaul

Your Production Stage Mapping

Pending → Preparing Material → Machineshop → Building → Prep & Coating → Ready to Ship → Shipped

+ Accounting status: Open Balance / Released  |  + Delivery confirmation status

Odoo mapping: Each stage becomes a routing operation on the manufacturing order, with assigned work centers, expected duration, quality checkpoints, barcode scan events, and labor time recording via timesheets. Manufacturing order states and custom Studio fields track accounting and delivery confirmation status alongside production progress.

Proposed Technical Approach

Configuration plan tailored to steel container manufacturing — roll-off containers, dumpsters, and custom builds.

Manufacturing (MRP)

  • Work centers defined per real station — material prep, CNC/machining, welding bays, paint booth, assembly area — not generic placeholders
  • Routings per product family: standard roll-off, dumpster, and custom build each with stage-specific operations and durations
  • BOMs structured by material type: steel sheets (with gauge tracking), structural parts, purchased hardware, welding consumables, paint/coating materials
  • Flexible consumption rules for leftover sheet metal — dedicated scrap/leftover locations, alternative component rules, and remanufacturing workflows for smaller parts cut from remnants
  • Capacity planning per work center with shift-aware scheduling

Inventory & Warehouse

  • Multi-location warehouse structure: raw steel storage, WIP locations per stage, finished goods, scrap/leftover inventory
  • Barcode operations for material issue, work order start/stop, inter-stage transfers, and shipping confirmation
  • Lot tracking for steel batches and traceability through production stages
  • Automated replenishment rules for consumables and purchased hardware

Quality, Maintenance & Labor

  • Quality checkpoints at weld inspection, coating adhesion test, and final assembly inspection — with non-conformance tracking and rework routing
  • Maintenance module — equipment registry tied to work centers, preventive maintenance schedules, machine downtime recording affecting capacity
  • Timesheets — labor cost allocation per work order and work center for efficiency reporting

Custom Fields (Odoo Studio)

  • Steel gauge, dimensions (L×W×H), weight per job, customer specifications — added via Studio with minimal customization overhead
  • Principle: use standard Odoo fields wherever possible; Studio only for industry-specific attributes that have no standard equivalent
